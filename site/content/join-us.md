# Join the Lab

We are always looking for talented, curious, and hard-working people to join us. We welcome applicants with interest or experience in one or more of the following:

* Population, comparative, and evolutionary genomics
* Genome assembly, variant calling, or other bioinformatics, especially with long-read data
* Scientific computing and programming (Python, R, shell, etc.) for large genomic datasets
* Natural history of drosophilids including fieldwork and specimen collection
* Molecular bench work and genomic library preps
* *Drosophila* genetics 

You do not need to know everything. Just have a clear idea of what you know and what you want to do, and bring a mind open to doing things that may not be in your usual wheelhouse. We are glad to help you build skills you don't have.

## Open positions

* **Prospective PhD students:** [apply](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/apply) to the [graduate program in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology](https://eeb.princeton.edu/graduate). As is convention in our field, it is highly recommended that you reach out to the PI beforehand.
* **Undergraduates:** Princeton undergraduates interested in research internships or senior thesis projects are encouraged to email.

## How to Apply

If you are interested in working with us, please send an email to the PI (Bernard) with the following information:
1. A brief (a few paragraphs) statement of your research interests and why you are interested in our lab.
2. Your current CV.
3. **Prospective PhDs and postdocs**: Contact information for 1-3 academic references.

*Note: To avoid getting caught in spam filters, please use a descriptive subject line such as "Prospective [PhD/Postdoc/Undergrad] Researcher - [Your Name]".*
