# Join the Lab

We are always looking for talented, curious, and hardworking people to join us. We welcome applicants with interest or experience in one or more of the following:

* Population, comparative, and evolutionary genomics
* Genome assembly, variant calling, or other bioinformatics, especially with long-read data
* Scientific computing and programming (Python, R, shell, etc.) for large genomic datasets
* Natural history of drosophilids including fieldwork and specimen collection
* Molecular bench work and genomic library preps
* *Drosophila* genetics 

No one needs to have everything-just a clear idea of what you want to do and an open mind to doing things not in your usual wheelhouse. We are glad to help you build the skills you don't yet have.

## Open positions

* **Prospective PhD students:** [apply](https://gradschool.princeton.edu/admission-onboarding/apply) through the [graduate program in Ecology and Evolutionary Biology](https://eeb.princeton.edu/graduate), and mention the lab in your application. It is highly recommended that you reach out beforehand.
* **Undergraduates:** Princeton undergraduates interested in research internships or senior thesis projects are encouraged to email.

## How to Apply

If you are interested in joining, please send an email to the PI (Bernard) with the following information:
1. A brief statement of your research interests and why you are interested in our lab.
2. Your current CV.
3. **Prospective PhDs and postdocs**: Contact information for 1-3 academic references.

*Note: To avoid getting caught in spam filters, please use a descriptive subject line such as "Prospective [PhD/Postdoc/Undergrad] Researcher - [Your Name]".*
